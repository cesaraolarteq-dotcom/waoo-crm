Recommendation: use **a condition on the assigned team**, so the member who drives may be the host, performer or other already-required role. Requiring host+driver in one fixed slot is useful only when the owner specifically wants that role to drive; it otherwise rejects a valid performer-driver team.

1. Existing staff requirement `{kind:"staff",quantity:1,skill:"host"}` remains unchanged.
2. Optional `skills_all:["host","driver"]` on a staff slot means the SAME assigned person must have every listed skill (AND), still quantity1. `skill` and `skills_all` are mutually exclusive; no OR interpretation or extra person. Validated known capability names, distinct bounded list. Legacy single skill compiles to the same one-element set.
3. Optional service `staff_constraints:[{kind:"assigned_staff_with_skills",skills_all:["driver"],minimum:1}]` means at least one of the people actually assigned to this service's existing staff slots must drive. This is not a staff/resource slot and adds0 staff/costume/equipment/vehicle units. Selected-but-unused spare drivers cannot satisfy it. One person occupies one simultaneous primary slot, but can satisfy the driving condition in addition to that role.

Example: existing requirements host1 + performer1; add only staff_constraints driver>=1. A host-driver + performer passes with2 staff. Host + performer-driver also passes with2. Host + performer without driver fails even if an unassigned spare driver was selected; do not auto-add a third person. A single host-driver service needs1 person. Costume-fit and exact outfit conditions remain simultaneous with these skills.

Matching must search for an assignment satisfying all slot AND-skills, costume compatibility and team constraints together, not greedily match roles and reject the first otherwise-valid crew. Carry service identity through flattened requirements so a driver assigned to another service cannot satisfy this one's constraint accidentally. Snapshot the compiled requirements and staff constraints in EventService/import/quote operational evidence; preserve old booked snapshots. Quantity/staff pricing never changes because a condition was added.

Do not confuse skill with transport feasibility: active driver capability does not prove a vehicle, seats/cargo, route, free driver time or actual driving assignment. Existing per-vehicle driver counts/seats, blocks, staff availability, transfers and45min buffer remain required. No policy-wide driver default until owner q28 is explicitly answered; missing capability/inventory remains unknown, not assumed available.
